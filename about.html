About

4+ years and 100+ A/B tests across two-sided marketplaces. I build the systems that make experimentation trustworthy: variance reduction (CUPED, MLRATE), sequential testing, automated SRM checks, and designs like synthetic control and BSTS for cases where a clean A/B test isn't possible.

Previously led product analytics at HeadHunter (hh.ru) and worked as a product analyst at OZON.ru. Taught a two-year course on product analytics and A/B testing at Moscow State University and spoke at Matemarketing 2024.
