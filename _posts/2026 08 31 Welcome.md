```python
import numpy as np

def mspre_ratio(y_true, y_pred_a, y_pred_b):
    """Ratio of mean squared prediction errors between two models."""
    mspe_a = np.mean((y_true - y_pred_a) ** 2)
    mspe_b = np.mean((y_true - y_pred_b) ** 2)
    return mspe_a / mspe_b
```
